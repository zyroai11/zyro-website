Getting started

  1. Open @ZyroAiAgent_bot on Telegram and press Start.
  2. Copy the contract address of a token on Robinhood Chain. It starts with 0x and has 42 characters.
  3. Send /scan followed by the address, for example /scan 0x7a3f…c21e.
  4. Read the report: the safety score, the market numbers and every warning that lowered the score.

There is nothing to install, and you do not need a wallet for a free scan. Prefer a browser? The web app runs the same scan.

Bot commands

/scan

Safety score and full report for a token. Add the contract address after the command.

/trending

Tokens getting attention on Robinhood Chain right now.

/link

Link your wallet so Zyro can read your $ZYRO balance. Add your public wallet address after the command.

/me

Your linked wallet, $ZYRO balance, tier and scans left today.

/start

The welcome message and the list of commands.

Web app

The web app at zyro-ai.xyz/app runs the full scan in your browser. There is no account and nothing to install.

  • Scan: paste a contract address. You get the score, the market numbers and all eight checks with the points each one took off.
  • Trending: tap any token in the trending list to scan it.
  • Agent: ask in plain words, for example "what's trending" or "how is the score made", or paste a contract.
  • Share: every report has a share link, such as zyro-ai.xyz/scan/0x…, that shows the score as a preview card.
  • Holder access: link a wallet that holds 100,000 $ZYRO to unlock AI analysis on every report.

A token that is still on a launchpad bonding curve has no DEX pool yet, so it cannot be scored until it graduates.

$ZYRO chart

The chart at zyro-ai.xyz/chart is built by Zyro from the chain itself. Every candle comes from buy and sell events on the $ZYRO curve contract on Pons.

  • Candle sizes: 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes and 1 hour. Times are UTC.
  • Switch between price and market cap.
  • The latest trades are listed next to the chart, each linked to its transaction.
  • It refreshes every 15 seconds.

Prices are shown in US dollars using the current ETH price, so older candles can differ slightly from their dollar value at the time.

Terminal agent

The terminal agent trades on Robinhood Chain with rules you set. It runs on your own machine, so your keys stay with you.

  1. Install it: npm install -g zyro-agent
  2. Create your settings: zyro init
  3. Check a token: zyro scan 0x…
  4. Try a buy in simulation: zyro buy 0x… 0.01
zyro buy

Scans the token, checks it against your rules, then buys. Simulated unless you add --live.

zyro sell

Sells a position you hold.

zyro positions

Open positions with cost, value and profit or loss.

zyro watch

Watches positions and sells when the stop loss or take profit is hit.

zyro chat

Give instructions in plain words.

zyro rules

Shows your current limits.

The default rules are a minimum score of 60, at most 0.05 ETH per trade, a 15% stop loss and a 50% take profit. Change them in ~/.zyro/.env.

Use a new wallet with a small balance. Live trading needs a private key in your settings file. Never share that file, and never use your main wallet.

How the score works

Every token starts at 100. Each check below that fails takes points off. The report lists every check that failed, so you always see why the score is what it is.

Check that failedPoints
Liquidity under $5K−30
Liquidity between $5K and $25K−15
More than 20 buys and zero sells in 24 hours (possible honeypot)−30
Sells more than three times buys in 24 hours−10
Pair is younger than 24 hours−15
Contract source is not verified on the explorer−15
FDV is more than 50 times the liquidity−10
24-hour volume is more than 20 times the liquidity−10
Fewer than 50 holders−10
No website or social links listed−10

If the explorer cannot return the holder count or the verification status, the score is capped at 90 and the report says so.

What the score means

75 to 100Low risk
50 to 74Medium risk
25 to 49High risk
0 to 24Very high risk

A high score is not a guarantee. It means the public data shows no obvious warning signs at the moment of the scan. Liquidity can be pulled and contracts can change later, so scan again before a large buy.

Becoming a holder

  1. Hold 100,000 $ZYRO or more in a wallet on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Send /link followed by that wallet's public address.
  3. Zyro reads your balance from the chain. Send /me to check your tier.

On the web, open the web app and paste the same public address under Holder access.

The AI analysis adds three short points to each report: the overall read, the biggest risk, and what to watch next.

Staying safe

  • Zyro will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Linking a wallet only needs the public address.
  • Zyro never messages you first. Anyone who does in Zyro's name is a scammer.
  • Only trust the links on this page. The official $ZYRO contract is published on zyro-ai.xyz first.
  • Treat the score as one input. Read the warnings, check the chart, and decide for yourself.

Where the data comes from

  • DexScreener: price, liquidity, FDV, volume, buys and sells, pair age, website and socials.
  • Blockscout, the Robinhood Chain explorer: holder count and contract verification.
  • Robinhood Chain RPC: your $ZYRO balance and every trade on the $ZYRO chart, read directly from the chain.
  • AI model: writes the holder analysis using only the facts in the report.
