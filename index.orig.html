    What Zyro reads on every Solana scan

    Each check can lower the score. The report lists every one that did, so you see the reason and not only the number.

    Liquidity

    How much money sits in the pool. Thin pools move violently and are easy to drain.

    Buys and sells

    Plenty of buys with zero sells is the classic sign of a honeypot, a token you cannot sell.

    Pair age

    Pairs younger than 24 hours get a warning. Most rug pulls happen early.

    FDV against liquidity

    A valuation far above the pool means the price is not backed by much.

    Volume

    Volume many times larger than liquidity often points to wash trading.

    Mint and freeze authority

    If the creator can still mint more tokens or freeze your wallet, the score drops hard.

    Top holders

    How much of the supply the top 10 wallets hold, and how many holders there are in total.

    Liquidity lock

    How much of the pool is locked or burned, so it cannot be pulled out from under you.

    Website and socials

    Whether the project lists any public presence at all.

    Where does the data come from?

    Market data for scans comes from DexScreener. On Solana, mint and freeze authority, top holders and the liquidity lock come from RugCheck. On Robinhood Chain, holder counts and contract verification come from Blockscout.

    Does the bot ever ask for my keys?

    Never. Anyone asking for a seed phrase or private key in Zyro's name is a scammer.
