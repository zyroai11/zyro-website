Privacy

Zyro is built to need as little about you as possible.

  • Website. Scanning needs no account. If you log in on the trade page, Privy creates your trading wallet and Zyro stores your open positions to run take profit and stop loss.
  • Telegram bot. The bot stores your Telegram user ID, the public wallet address you link and your daily scan count, so it can apply the free limit and holder access.
  • Agent messages. Questions you send to the web agent or AI analysis may be passed to an AI model provider to write the answer. Do not include personal information.
  • Analytics and logs. We count page views without cookies. Our host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security.
  • Never collected. Seed phrases and private keys. Zyro will never ask for them.
  • Other services. Market and chain data comes from DexScreener, RugCheck, Blockscout and the Robinhood Chain RPC. Links to StonkFun, Telegram and X follow their own policies.

To have your bot data removed, message @zyroxstonk on X.
