How an order works

  1. Connect your wallet

    Any wallet that supports Robinhood Chain. Zyro never holds your funds.

  2. Pick a market

    A Stock Token to buy, or a perp to go long or short with USDG margin.

  3. Read the Zyro check

    What the token is, and for perps, how far the price can move before liquidation.

  4. Confirm in your wallet

    The order goes to Uniswap or Lighter on Robinhood Chain.

Good to know

Stock Tokens and perps are not offered in some countries, including the US, UK and Canada. The trade page will check your region before you can place an order.

What is a Stock Token?

A token issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited that tracks the price of a US stock. It is a debt security, not the share itself, so it carries no voting rights.

What do I use as margin for perps?

USDG, the dollar stablecoin used by Lighter on Robinhood Chain.

What is liquidation?

If the price moves against a leveraged position far enough, the position is closed and the margin is lost. Higher leverage means a smaller move does it.

Can I trade on the website?

Yes. Open Trade to buy $ZYRO, any Robinhood Chain token or a Stock Token with an automatic take profit and stop loss. Perps through Lighter are being built now.
